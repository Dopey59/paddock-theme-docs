Sections and blocks

Every page is built from sections. In the theme editor, click Add section to add one, and drag sections to reorder them. This page lists the sections made for parts stores, then the general ones.

Made for parts stores

Section What it does Tips
Vehicle hero Headline with a Year → Make → Model → Engine picker (and an optional part-number search). The chosen vehicle is saved in the shopper's garage Add up to three key figures (rating, cutoff hour, guarantee). Choose the collection that opens after a vehicle is picked
Cinematic tour One photo of a car or bike; while visitors scroll, the camera zooms on each part and shows the products for it Use a landscape photo at least 2,400 px wide. See Cinematic tour in the setup guide
Shop the build A build photo with numbered hotspots, the list of parts with install difficulty and time, and an "Add the full build" button The photo keeps its proportions so hotspots stay on the parts at every screen size
Category tiles Collections as image tiles with their number of parts Leave the title and image empty to use the collection's own
Trust bar Short reassurance items with icons (fitment guarantee, same-day shipping, returns) Four items read best
Scrolling text A moving band of short messages Visitors can pause it
Before / after Two photos with a slider, for example stock and modified Use two photos of the same size and framing
Key figures Numbers that count up when they come into view Write values as you want them shown: 480,000, 98% or 4.8/5
Promo banner A highlighted offer, for example a trade account, with bullet points and buttons

General sections

Image banner, Image with text, Slideshow, Multicolumn, Rich text, Video, Featured collection, Featured product, Blog posts, Testimonials, Logo list, FAQ / collapsible content, Email sign-up and Custom Liquid.

Frames: the Vehicle hero, Image banner, Image with text, Slideshow, Video, Before / after and Shop the build sections can use a Rounded or Notched frame. The notched frame is a rounded panel with cut-outs for a tab (text or logo), a caption and a "Scroll to discover" cue.

Product page blocks

Block What it shows
Fitment check "Fits your…", "Doesn't fit your…" or "Check fitment", with a link to change vehicle
Fitment table Every vehicle the part fits, with a search box when the list is long. The shopper's vehicle is highlighted
Install difficulty and time A 1-to-5 meter and the time, from the custom.install_difficulty and custom.install_time metafields
Installation request A short form sent through the Shopify contact form, with the product, the vehicle and the ZIP code
Same-day shipping countdown "Order within 2 h 14 min, ships today". Turn it on in Theme settings → Shipping only if it is true
Inventory status In stock, "Only N left" below the threshold you set, or available on backorder
Volume pricing Price breaks from Shopify B2B catalogs
Price, Variant picker, Buy buttons, SKU, Brand, Description, Collapsible tab, Pickup availability, Pay in installments, Guarantees The usual product information

Star ratings show on product cards and under the product title when a review app fills Shopify's standard product rating metafields (reviews.rating and reviews.rating_count). No setting is needed.

Complete the job: the product recommendations section can show complementary parts as a kit with an "Add selected" button. Choose the complementary products in the free Search & Discovery app.
