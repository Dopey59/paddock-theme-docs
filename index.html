Paddock documentation

Paddock is a Shopify theme for stores that sell car and motorcycle parts. Shoppers save their vehicle once, and every page, product card and cart line tells them whether a part fits it. No fitment app, no monthly subscription.

Start here

  1. Set up your store (about 20 minutes): one metaobject, one product metafield and one filter.
  2. Import your fitment data if you already have it in a spreadsheet, a TecDoc export or an ACES file.
  3. Build your pages with the sections made for parts stores: vehicle search hero, cinematic tour, Shop the build and more.

Four styles

Paddock includes four styles. You pick one when you add the theme, and you can switch later in Theme settings → Style without losing your content.

Style Made for Look
Grand Tourer Performance parts for sports cars and grand tourers Dark, night-drive look, italic racing headings
Service Bay Auto parts distributors serving drivers and trade customers Light and calm, built for large catalogs
Superbike Sportbike parts and track-day gear Red on black, race-paddock energy
Overland Adventure motorcycle protection, luggage and lighting Warm sand and teal, travel storytelling
