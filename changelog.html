Release notes

1.0.0

First release on the Shopify Theme Store.

  • Vehicle fitment built in: Year → Make → Model → Engine picker, saved multi-vehicle garage, fitment badges on product cards, fitment panel and table on product pages, vehicle reminder on each cart line.
  • Fitment data on Shopify's own objects: a vehicle metaobject and a custom.fitment product metafield, plus an offline converter to import spreadsheets, TecDoc exports or ACES files.
  • "Only show parts that fit" across the whole collection with the Search & Discovery Fitment filter.
  • Sections for parts stores: Vehicle hero, Cinematic tour, Shop the build, Category tiles, Trust bar, Before / after, Key figures and Promo banner, with optional notched frames.
  • Selling tools: same-day shipping countdown, free shipping progress bar, low stock message, install difficulty and time, installation request, complementary parts kit, volume pricing and star ratings from review apps.
  • Navigation: dropdown menus on three levels in a full-width panel, keyboard accessible, and country/region and language selectors in the footer when you sell in several countries or languages.
  • Four styles: Grand Tourer, Showroom, Superbike and Trail, each with a Racing or Classic look.
  • Accessibility and performance: WCAG 2.1 AA contrast kept automatically when you change colors, keyboard navigation, reduced-motion support, and main images loaded first.
  • Languages: English and French, storefront and theme editor.
